Backup & Sync

God Focus works without an account. An account is optional and exists to protect Scripture progress and restore it after reinstalling the App or moving to a new iPhone.

Signed-in users can back up portable progress using Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Signing out does not delete Scripture progress stored locally on your iPhone.

Open God Focus → Settings → Backup & Sync to sign in, back up, restore, or sign out.

Restore progress

On the God Focus welcome screen, tap:

Already use God Focus? Restore progress

Then sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used previously. God Focus will check for a cloud backup and guide you through restore if one is available.

You can also restore from God Focus → Settings → Backup & Sync after signing in.

Delete Account

If you signed in for Backup & Sync:

  1. Open God Focus
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Open Backup & Sync
  4. Tap Delete Account

This deletes your cloud backup and sign-in. Scripture progress on this iPhone stays unless you clear it separately.

Designed for iPhone. Requires iOS 17.0 or later. Available in English and Spanish. No account is required to begin.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. God Focus works without an account. Sign in only if you want optional Backup & Sync for portable Scripture progress.

What is a Faith Pause?

A Faith Pause is a scheduled time when the apps you chose to protect are paused with Apple Screen Time. When you start the authorized Faith Pause from a blocked-app flow, God Focus is designed to move through Scripture, then prayer, then unlock.

Why does God Focus need Screen Time access?

God Focus uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity APIs to pause the apps you select at your Faith Pause times. Without that permission, the App cannot apply a Screen Time shield to those apps.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open God Focus → Settings → Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. App Store entitlements stay with Apple, separate from optional Backup & Sync.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open God Focus → Settings and use Manage Subscription, or go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting God Focus does not cancel an Apple subscription.

What happens if I reinstall the app?

Set up Faith Pauses and Screen Time access again. Eligible purchases can be restored with the same Apple ID. If you previously used Backup & Sync, use Restore progress on the welcome screen or Settings → Backup & Sync to restore portable Scripture progress.

How is my information handled?

Most Faith Pause and Bible data stays on your device. Optional Backup & Sync uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. PostHog is used for privacy-minimized product analytics, Superwall for paywalls, and Apple for purchases. Read the Privacy Policy.
