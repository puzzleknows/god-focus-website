3. The App

God Focus is a spiritual and productivity tool. It helps you put Scripture and prayer before distracting apps through scheduled Faith Pauses. You choose protected apps using Apple Screen Time, choose Faith Pause times, and, when a pause is active, selected apps may be paused with Apple’s Screen Time shield.

When you start an authorized Faith Pause from a blocked-app flow, the App is designed to move through Scripture, then prayer, then unlock. The App also includes Bible reading, Discover Scripture, 90 Days with Scripture, streaks, saved verses, and optional Faith Reminders.

God Focus does not require a user account or social profile.
