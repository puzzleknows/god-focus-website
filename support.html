Common Questions

Why is an app not being paused?

Open Unlock with Prayer, go to Locks, confirm the app is selected, and verify that Screen Time / Family Controls permission is enabled. Some Screen Time changes may require reopening the App or reselecting apps.

I did not receive the prayer notification.

Open iPhone Settings → Notifications → Unlock with Prayer and allow notifications. Then return to the App and try the blocked-app flow again.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the App’s Settings or paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Unlock with Prayer, then choose the available cancellation option. Deleting the App does not automatically cancel a subscription.

What is Emergency Unlock?

Emergency Unlock is a fallback intended to help you regain access when you cannot complete the normal prayer flow. Availability and behavior may depend on the current version of the App.

How is my data handled?

Read our Privacy Policy. The current version is designed to keep app selections, prayer progress, reminders, and related settings on your device.
