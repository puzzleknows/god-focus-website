Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oleg Pyshenkin (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the iOS app Unlock with Prayer (the “App”).

1. Summary

Unlock with Prayer is designed to work primarily on your device. The current version does not require an account and does not use first-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • app preferences and settings;
  • selected Screen Time apps and blocking configuration;
  • prayer progress, streaks, achievements, and journey progress;
  • Faith Reminder and notification settings;
  • Bible reading progress and selected topics;
  • temporary unlock and re-lock state used by the app-blocking flow.

This information is used to operate the App and personalize your experience. It is not uploaded to a first-party server in the current version.

3. Screen Time and Family Controls

The App uses Apple’s Screen Time, Family Controls, Device Activity, and Managed Settings frameworks so you can choose apps to pause and temporarily unlock them after completing a prayer pause. Your selected apps are represented and processed through Apple’s system frameworks and are kept on your device. We do not receive a readable list of your installed apps on a remote server.

4. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the App may send local notifications for prayer prompts, blocked-app flows, and Faith Reminders. Notification settings and reminder content are handled on your device.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your full payment card details. The App may receive transaction and entitlement information from Apple so it can determine whether premium access is active and restore purchases.

6. Support Communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any screenshots or diagnostics you attach. We use this information only to respond to your request, investigate issues, and improve support.

7. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple services, including the App Store, StoreKit, Screen Time / Family Controls, and local notifications. Apple processes information under its own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until you remove it through the App, reset the relevant settings, or delete the App. Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your request, maintain records, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. No account or personal profile is required in the current version. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

10. Your Choices

  • You may decline Screen Time or notification permission, although some features will not work.
  • You may change notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You may manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
  • You may delete locally stored App data by deleting the App or using any reset controls made available in the App.
  • You may contact us with privacy questions or requests.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page.
