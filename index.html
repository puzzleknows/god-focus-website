Unlock with Prayer helps you pause selected applications before opening them, complete a short prayer and Scripture reflection, and build faith-focused habits on your iPhone.

About the App

Unlock with Prayer is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 17.0 or later. No account or login is required. A functional free tier is available, and an optional Pro subscription unlocks additional features.

  • Designed for iPhone
  • Requires iOS 17.0 or later
  • No user account required
  • Free tier and optional Pro subscription

Features

  • Prayer Locks — Pause selected apps and unlock them temporarily after completing a prayer pause.
  • Scripture and prayer pauses — Short reflections to help you pause before opening distracting apps.
  • Scheduled Times and Every App Open — Choose when Prayer Locks apply: on a schedule or each time a selected app is opened.
  • Bible reading — Read Scripture, explore topics, and track reading progress on your device.
  • Journey, streaks, and achievements — Follow your faith journey and celebrate consistent prayer habits.
  • Faith Reminders — Optional local reminders to help you stay consistent.
  • Pro subscription — Optional paid access to additional features, managed through the App Store.
